# Acceptance and Verification Gates

Version: v0.1.1

Release: Public

## 1. Purpose

These gates determine whether a concrete customer integration may be formally accepted.

All applicable gates must pass.

## 2. Acceptance gates

| ID | Scope | Requirement | Pass condition | Failure boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ACCEPT-01` | `CUSTOMER_MAPPING` | SEMANTIC_ROLE_MAPPING_NOT_REFERENCE_FIELD_NAME_COPYING | EVERY_SELECTED_CUSTOMER_ROLE_BINDS_TO_CUSTOMER_OWN_FIELD_OR_EQUIVALENT_REPRESENTATION_WITHOUT_REQUIRING_REFERENCE_FIELD_NAMES | `HOLD_MAPPING_CONTRACT` |
| `ACCEPT-02` | `CUSTOMER_REQUIRED` | REQUIRED_ROLE_BOUND_WHEN_APPLICABLE | EACH_CAPABILITY_SCOPED_REQUIRED_ROLE_HAS_FIELD_SOURCE_AND_PROVENANCE_BINDING_WHEN_APPLICABILITY_IS_TRUE | `FAIL_OR_HOLD_REQUIRED_INPUT_GATE` |
| `ACCEPT-03` | `CUSTOMER_OPTIONAL` | OPTIONAL_ROLE_NOT_SILENTLY_PROMOTED | OPTIONAL_ROLE_MAY_REMAIN_UNBOUND_UNLESS_CUSTOMER_MAPPING_CONTRACT_EXPLICITLY_REQUIRES_IT | `FAIL_CONTRACT_PROMOTION_GATE` |
| `ACCEPT-04` | `AUTHORITY_BOUNDARY` | CUSTOMER_FACT_NOT_GOVERNANCE_STATE | CUSTOMER_SUPPLIED_VALUES_REMAIN_CUSTOMER_FACT_AUTHORITY_AND_ARE_NOT_ACCEPTED_AS_GOVERNANCE_DERIVED_OR_GENERATED_AUTHORITY | `FAIL_AUTHORITY_BOUNDARY_GATE` |
| `ACCEPT-05` | `GOVERNANCE_DERIVED` | DERIVED_STATE_PROTECTED | CUSTOMER_CANNOT_SUPPLY_GOVERNANCE_DERIVED_ROLE_AS_AUTHORITATIVE_GOVERNANCE_STATE | `FAIL_GOVERNANCE_DERIVATION_AUTHORITY_GATE` |
| `ACCEPT-06` | `GOVERNANCE_GENERATED` | GENERATED_ARTIFACT_PROTECTED | CUSTOMER_CANNOT_FORGE_OR_SUPPLY_GOVERNANCE_GENERATED_ROLE_AS_AUTHORITATIVE_ARTIFACT | `FAIL_GOVERNANCE_GENERATION_AUTHORITY_GATE` |
| `ACCEPT-07` | `FALLBACK` | FALLBACK_EXPLICIT_ONLY | ANY_FALLBACK_IS_DECLARED_IN_THE_CUSTOMER_INPUT_MAPPING_CONTRACT_AND_DOES_NOT_REWRITE_CUSTOMER_FACT_AUTHORITY | `FAIL_FALLBACK_AUTHORITY_GATE` |
| `ACCEPT-08` | `PROVENANCE` | SOURCE_TRANSFORM_PROVENANCE_EXPLICIT | EVERY_BOUND_CUSTOMER_ROLE_IDENTIFIES_SOURCE_AND_PROVENANCE_AND_DECLARES_TRANSFORM_WHEN_A_TRANSFORM_EXISTS | `HOLD_TRACEABILITY_GATE` |
| `ACCEPT-09` | `PORTABILITY` | REFERENCE_IMPLEMENTATION_NOT_REQUIRED | CUSTOMER_FIELD_NAMES_SOURCE_LAYOUT_RUNTIME_ARCHITECTURE_AND_REFERENCE_ROLE_CARDINALITY_ARE_NOT_REQUIRED_TO_MATCH_THE_REFERENCE_IMPLEMENTATION | `FAIL_SYSTEM_AGNOSTIC_BOUNDARY_GATE` |
| `ACCEPT-10` | `OBSERVATION_MODEL` | REFERENCE_OBSERVATION_AXES_NOT_FIXED | CUSTOMER_GOVERNANCE_OBSERVATION_MODEL_MAY_USE_CAPABILITY_APPROPRIATE_METRICS_AND_DOES_NOT_REQUIRE_X_Y_Z | `FAIL_REFERENCE_MODEL_PROMOTION_GATE` |
| `ACCEPT-11` | `CARDINALITY` | ROLE_CARDINALITY_IMPLEMENTATION_DEFINED | CUSTOMER_MAPPING_CARDINALITY_IS_DECLARED_BY_THE_CUSTOMER_CONTRACT_AND_IS_NOT_INFERRED_FROM_REFERENCE_ROW_COUNT | `FAIL_REFERENCE_CARDINALITY_PROMOTION_GATE` |
| `ACCEPT-12` | `OBSERVATION_HANDOFF` | OBSERVATION_LOCATOR_RETURNED_AND_EXECUTION_BOUND | EACH_ACCEPTED_OBSERVABLE_EXECUTION_RETURNS_OR_SURFACES_AN_IMPLEMENTATION_DEFINED_EXECUTION_OR_SESSION_IDENTITY_AND_A_READ_ONLY_OBSERVATION_LOCATOR_BOUND_TO_THE_SAME_EXECUTION_WITHOUT_REQUIRING_INTERNAL_ID_DISCOVERY_GUESSING_OR_WRITE_CREDENTIALS | `HOLD_OBSERVATION_HANDOFF_GATE` |

## 3. Formal close rule

A customer integration must not be formally closed while any applicable acceptance gate is failed or unresolved.

## 4. Minimum evidence

Acceptance evidence should identify:

- customer mapping contract version;
- integration adapter version;
- governance capability set;
- input source/provenance evidence;
- applicable fallback declarations;
- governance output/evidence identity;
- read-only observation verification;
- execution/session identity and returned observation locator or equivalent read-only observation reference;
- evidence that the observation locator resolves to the same accepted execution/session identity;
- gate-by-gate result;
- final sealed acceptance result.
